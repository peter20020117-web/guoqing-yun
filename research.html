Peace Road Archive

Minimalist Compositional Techniques as a Sonic Archive of Restorative Experience

A practice-based research project exploring how minimalist compositional techniques can transform personal experiences in natural environments into a sonic archive. Through field recordings, sound collage, repetition, gradual change, and instrumental composition, the project traces a restorative journey from anxiety to awareness and inner balance across three works.
